Series variety: Argentine Spanish (Rioplatense), voseo. Reviewer: Claude. This is NOT a native speaker's judgement, so the risk is accepted knowingly, reduced by the rules below.

## Rules that keep the risk small
1. Only high-confidence A1 phrases that are standard across Spanish-speaking countries, or clearly Argentine (voseo) when the variety differs.
2. Every line is listed here with a verdict before its lesson may be marked `auto-reviewed`. A phrase Claude is not sure about is cut, never published with a guess.
3. Whisper reads every spoken clip back against the script on every build (catches wrong words and bad pronunciation, not subtle accent).
4. Corrections are welcome and count as engagement (owner, 2026-10-05): a viewer who corrects us is a comment, not a problem. Pinned-comment line for every video: "Argentine Spanish. Spot a mistake or a better way to say it? Tell us." When someone is right, fix the lesson file, rebuild, replace the video and reply "Thanks, corrected."
5. Regional notes are said in the lesson itself ("Argentines say vos").

## Known limits of this review
- Respellings (e.g. "meh SHAH-moh") approximate the Argentine "sh" sound for English readers; they are teaching aids, not phonetic transcriptions.
- Aspirated or dropped final "s" in fast Argentine speech is not modelled.
- Planned but unwritten phrases in `data/curriculum.json` are drafts and get this table entry only when their lesson is written.
